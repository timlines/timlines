The greatest community in the world — tribute

Hey #100Devs family! I wanted to share something that hit me pretty hard this week.

A few years ago, I joined 100Devs because I wanted to become a software developer.

Since then, the road has not exactly been straight.

I learned full-stack development. I built projects. I worked professionally in QA at Cloud Imperium Games. I got laid off. Eventually I found myself working long days as an HVAC technician while still trying to build software, study, learn AI, and somehow find my way back into tech.

There have been plenty of times when I wondered whether I was still really a software developer at all.

Then this week I went to Commit Your Code.

I got to reconnect with Leon, spend time around developers, builders, engineers, and people from this community, and talk about the things I’ve been building and learning.

And somewhere in the middle of all of that, something clicked:

I am a software developer.

Not “I want to be one someday.”

I am one today.

And I realized something else too.

One of the most valuable things 100Devs gave me wasn’t JavaScript, React, Node, interview prep, or even the ability to build software.

It gave me a community.

Learning to code is hard. Breaking into tech is hard. Getting knocked down and trying to come back is hard. And sometimes your own motivation isn’t enough to carry you indefinitely.

You need people.

People who remind you what you’re capable of.

People who are building beside you.

People who will answer a question, make an introduction, celebrate a win, or tell you to keep going when the road gets long.

At CYC, I realized:

I found my people. I found my tribe. And I found my purpose.

So for anyone in #100Devs who’s struggling right now, disappeared for a while, got a different job, didn’t get hired as quickly as you hoped, or feels like everyone else passed you by:

Your story isn’t over.

Keep learning.

Keep building.

Keep talking to people.

Help somebody else when you can.

And stay connected to the community.

Because I’ve become convinced that none of us are supposed to do this alone.

Thank you, Leon. And thank you to everyone in #100Devs who has been part of this journey.

Community might just be our greatest strength.

#100Devs 🛶🛶🪵🪵

Keep building.

“This is not the real community, this is just a tribute.”
